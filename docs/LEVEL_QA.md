## Chapters

1. **First Orbit**: Launch, capture and reach the portal.
2. **Twin Suns**: Several wells pull at once. Pick your route.
3. **Satellite Alley**: Time your launch around orbiting satellites.
4. **Laser Observatory**: Read the warning, then cross the sweeping beam.
5. **Wandering Worlds**: Planets drift. Aim where they will be.
6. **Reverse Gravity**: Inside the marked zones, pulls become pushes.
7. **Pulse Fields**: Emitters shove you away on a rhythm.
8. **Folded Space**: Wormholes carry your momentum across the map.
9. **Locked Constellations**: Collect shards to open gates and the portal.
10. **Orbital Machines**: Several hazards run together. Stay precise.
11. **Starship Trials**: Long routes that reward efficient launches.
12. **Event Horizon**: Everything you have learned, at once.
